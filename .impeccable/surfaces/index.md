## Direction contract

THESIS: The homepage is a notebook page of pasted notes, not a pixel dashboard of identical tool tiles.

OWN-WORLD: Hand-drawn doodle. Paper #fffef5, ink #2c2c2c, marker red #ff6b6b, teal #4ecdc4, yellow #ffd93d. Dashed 2px ink borders, rounded-sm only, hard marker-offset shadows. Patrick Hand and ZCOOL KuaiLe. Notebook rules, red margin, spiral holes, tape, pins, clips.

STORY: The visitor scans tool names on a notebook, taps a note, and leaves for that tool. Copy and destinations stay as they are.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Title 小工具集 with a yellow marker slash, then the note grid. Fitness is the first note.

FORM: Notebook of notes, position 1 of 1. seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
