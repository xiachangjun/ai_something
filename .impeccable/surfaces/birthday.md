## Direction contract

THESIS: A notebook of birthday slips: unlock, scan who is next, add or tear one off. Not a pixel lock screen.

OWN-WORLD: Same doodle as the homepage. Paper #fffef5, ink #2c2c2c, marker red/teal/yellow. Dashed 2px borders, rounded-sm, hard teal/red offsets. ZCOOL KuaiLe on chrome, Patrick Hand on dates.

STORY: The visitor unlocks, reads names ordered by next birthday, adds a solar or lunar date, or deletes a slip.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Locked: back link, title 生日记录, password field, 进入. Unlocked: back, title, 添加, then the next-birthday list.

FORM: Login then list, position 1 of 1. seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
